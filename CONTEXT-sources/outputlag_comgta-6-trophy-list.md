# GTA 6 Trophy List: Every Trophy and Achievement

By Steven Mills · Updated Nov 19, 2026 · Verified on Launch version, checked November 19, 2026

## The short answer

How many

55 trophies on PS5: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze. Xbox Series X|S has the same 55 achievements worth 1,000 Gamerscore. 28 of them are hidden.

The Platinum

Vice Got a Price! for earning every other trophy. On Xbox it should be worth 0G, since the other 54 achievements add up to the full 1,000. The two Golds are A Long Road (100% completion, 100G) and You Gotta Know When to Get Off (the final mission, 50G).

Missable

Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 counts toward Positive Mental Attitude and disappears after that chapter. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

How long

Around 200 hours for the Platinum. No online trophies, no difficulty trophies and no mission medals: it takes ages, but none of it is hard. Cheats should switch off trophies and saving.

## Save before every chapter: GTA 6 has no mission replay

You can finish the whole list in one playthrough. These four rules keep you from needing a second one.

- No mission replay, no New Game Plus. Once a story mission is done you can’t select it again. The game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave: keep one at the start of each chapter and an untouched one from just before the final mission, and rotate the rest.
- Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1. After Lucia is home, check the kitchen in the morning (a morning after the mission Down to Earth is the usual time). She heads out to the balcony of Jason’s house to meditate. Follow her and accept the optional task. It’s gone once Chapter 1 ends. Nobody has confirmed that Positive Mental Attitude strictly needs it, but do it anyway and keep a Chapter 1 save until the trophy pops.
- Do Yago’s timed race the moment it appears. This Chapter 3 race in Yago’s street racing strand (it should be called Race Against Yago) carries an hourglass icon, and the strand counts toward Strangers & Freaks (every strand is in our side missions guide). Save first, then race.
- Never use cheats on your trophy save. Cheats should switch off both trophies and saving.

Everything outside the story stays available after the credits, including the side-story finales and the 100% checklist. That checklist only appears after the story, under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion, but work you do earlier still counts. Our GTA 6 walkthrough lists the main missions in order if you want to plan your chapter saves.

Stunts count toward Cunning Stunts. You need 250 for the trophy and 300 for 100%.
