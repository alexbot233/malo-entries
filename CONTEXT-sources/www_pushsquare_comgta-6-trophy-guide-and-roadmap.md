# GTA 6: Trophy Guide & Roadmap (WIP)

[**GTA 6**](https://www.pushsquare.com/games/ps5/grand_theft_auto_6) is an enormous open world game with an unprecedented amount of activities to engage with. To unlock the Platinum Trophy, you'll need to see and do *almost* everything, so expect to set aside up to 300 hours or more. In this spoiler-free [**GTA 6 guide**](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-guide), we're going to explain what you need to do to get all the Trophies.

> This page is a work in progress. So far, the rough Trophy roadmap and all Trophies have been listed. Instructions for more than half the Trophies has also been included. This page will be updated with more detailed unlock instructions for every Trophy in time.

## GTA 6 Trophy Roadmap

[![](https://images.pushsquare.com/0cc66b19e8ed5/1280x720.jpg)](https://images.pushsquare.com/0cc66b19e8ed5/large.jpg)

- **Estimated Trophy Difficulty:** 4/10
- **Approximate Time to Platinum:** 250+ Hours
- **Offline Trophies:** 55
- **Online Trophies:** 0
- **Missable Trophies:** None known yet
- **Glitched Trophies:** None known yet
- **Hardest Trophy:** '[A Long Road](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-trophy-guide-and-roadmap#a-long-road)' (It'll take you a long time!)
- **Minimum Playthroughs**: 1
- **Can You Continue Playing After Credits?**: Yes
- **Does Difficulty Affect Trophies?**: No, can enable [Accessible Combat](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-tips-and-tricks-you-need-to-know#how-do-you-change-the-difficulty) and [Reduced Vehicle Damage](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-tips-and-tricks-you-need-to-know#how-do-you-change-the-difficulty)
- **Do Cheats Affect Trophies?**: Yes, using [cheats will disable Trophies](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-cheats-and-unlock-conditions#do-cheats-disable-trophies)
- **Are There Manual Saves?**: Yes, 15 slots plus autosave

### Step 1: Complete the Story Missions

GTA 6 is the most anticipated PS5 game ever, so I'd recomment you begin your quest for the Platinum Trophy by simply enjoying the story at your leisure.

You'll unlock a good number of Trophies by completing the campaign.

Feel free to engage in any activities that take your interest along the way. You can track your progress with most activities at any time by pressing Touch Pad > **Progress**.

You'll also unlock a [**100% Completion Checklist**](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-100percent-completion-checklist) upon finishing the story, which will help you to clean up any outstanding activities later.

### Step 2: 100% Completion Clean Up

Once you've completed GTA 6's main story and unlocked '[**You Gotta Know When to Get Off**](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-trophy-guide-and-roadmap#you-gotta-know-when-to-get-off)', you'll be able to continue to explore Leonida freely for as long as you like. This is when the real work begins towards your [**100% Completion Checklist**](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-100percent-completion-checklist).

While you don't need to do absolutely everything to achieve 100% completion, you do have to do *almost* everything. For example, there are 18 sets of [**Collectible Cards**](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-collectible-card-locations) to find, and you'll need 15 full sets to contribute towards 100% completion.

Once you've achieved 100% completion, you'll likely have unlocked all of the Trophies, although some outstanding gameplay challenges may remain. Clean those up to gain your coveted Platinum Trophy.

### [Cunning Stunts](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-trophy-guide-and-roadmap#cunning-stunts)

**Silver**

- **Complete 250 Stunts.**

**Stunts** are a type of activity in GTA 6 that consist of several actions (the total number of each type is listed in parentheses):

- **Stunt Jumps** Jump off ramps, bridges, construction sites, and more. Test your skills using specific vehicles, and attempt aerial tricks. (130)
- **Under the Bridges** Fly your aircraft through tight gaps under bridges and emerge unscathed on the other side. (202)
- **Knife Flights** Flip your airplane 90 degress and slip through narrow gaps between taal buildings around Vice City. (40)
- **Base Jumps** Leap off the tallest peaks and buildings in Leonida, and drop through checkpoints as fast as possible before parachuting onto the target. (25)

In total there are **397 stunts** in the game, so you need to complete a little over half of them to unlock this Trophy.

Keep in mind that there are additional challenges attached to **Stunt Jumps** specifically which don't necessarily need to be cleared for this Trophy.

You can see all the stunts you've discovered on the map by pressing Touch Pad **> Exploration > Stunt Spots**.

You can also track your progress by pressing Touch Pad **> Progress > Activities > Stunts**.

### [The Shootist](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-trophy-guide-and-roadmap#the-shootist)

**Silver**

- **Earn a gold medal in all Shooting Range challenges.**

You need to earn a gold medal in **42 Shooting Range challenges** to unlock this Trophy.

There are two shooting range locations in Leonida:

- **Rodgers Ranch & Shooting Range** (Port Gellhorn)
- **Griffin Shooting Range** (Balsero)

You can find the locations in your map by pressing Touch Pad **> Activities > Shooting Ranges**.

Most challenges have three different difficulty tiers you'll need to complete and earn a gold medal in. Some challenges are locked until you fulfil other conditions, which you can view in your map.

You keep tabs on your Shooting Range progress, you can press Touch Pad **> Activities > Shooting Ranges**.

### [Earn Cash, Not Kudos](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-trophy-guide-and-roadmap#earn-cash-not-kudos)

**Bronze**

- **Win 20 different races.**

You'll encounter many race locations as you explore Leonida.

You can keep track of them all by pressing Touch Pad **> Activities > Racing**.

Most race locations will allow you to hire cars; pay attention to your car's **Rating** if you're planning to use your own. (It can be improved by purchasing upgrades from **Mod Shops**.)

Do keep in mind that this Trophy requires you to win 20 *different* races; you won't unlock it by repeatedly winning the same one.

### [Little Boxes](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-trophy-guide-and-roadmap#little-boxes)

**Bronze**

- **Purchase all garage properties.**

**Garages** allow you to store vehicles you've registered at a **Pay 'N' Spray** or **Mod Shop**. This means your vehicle will be saved for future use and can be called using **Scooter Bros**.

Press Up to access your smartphone and select the **Vice City Real Estate** app, as shown:

[![](https://images.pushsquare.com/d963394a217d5/1280x720.jpg)](https://images.pushsquare.com/d963394a217d5/large.jpg)

There are **8 garages** you need to buy for this Trophy, all with 1 parking spot:

1. **Mitchel Auto Center:** $4,000
2. **7th Avenue Garage:** $12,000
3. **Vela Rota Drive Garage:** $24,000
4. **Persistence Road Garage:** $7,600
5. **Wyatt Motors:** $21,600
6. **Bonita Court Garage:** $34,000
7. **Atlantic Court Garage:** $40,000
8. **Store n' Bolt:** $16,800

So in total you need to spend **$160,000** to buy all these garages, adding a total of 8 vehicle storage slots to your game. (You can add even more via the [**Ultimate Edition**](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-how-to-get-ultimate-editon-items-and-pre-order-bonuses) and **GTA+**.)

### [On the Spot](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-trophy-guide-and-roadmap#on-the-spot)

**Bronze**

- **Register 10 vehicles to the Scooter Bros app.**

**Scooter Bros** is an in-game service which will deliver fully repaired vehicles you've registered to your current location for a small fee.

Vehicles you're driving can be registered at a **Pay 'N' Spray** or **Mod Shop** at any time, as long as you have enough empty parking spots. The fee varies depending on the value of the vehicle:

[![](https://images.pushsquare.com/63345522b389e/1280x720.jpg)](https://images.pushsquare.com/63345522b389e/large.jpg)

You can get more parking spots by purchasing more garages from the **Vice City Real Estate** app.

You need to register 10 different vehicles to unlock this Trophy.
