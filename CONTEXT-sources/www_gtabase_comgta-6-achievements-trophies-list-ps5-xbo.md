## GTA 6 Trophy Roadmap

- Time to Platinum: around 200 hours
- Trophies: 55 on PS5 (1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze) and 55 Achievements worth 1,000G on Xbox Series X|S
- Online or DLC trophies: none, everything is single-player
- Playthroughs: one. There are no difficulty settings, no New Game+ and no mission replay, but free roam carries on after the story
- Missable: possibly Positive Mental Attitude (a Chapter 1 meditation with Lucia); everything else can be done after the story
- Cheats: using a cheat disables trophies and saving, so keep them off your trophy run
- Hardest trophy: A Long Road (100% Completion)

### Step 1: Play the story, and side content as it shows up

Play through the five chapters and take on optional missions and character quests as they appear, since many are only offered for a while. Along the way, scan vehicles with the WAiNK app (200 are needed for 100%), study animals with binoculars (100), meditate with both characters and turn on every progress and collectible notification in the settings. In Chapter 1, don't skip Lucia's balcony meditation, and steer clear of the Irredeemable criminal profile: it's permanent.

### Step 2: Finish the 100% checklist

After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work through what's left: every robbery, bank vault and gang operation, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races, Bret's Target Decks and 70 of the 80 challenges.
