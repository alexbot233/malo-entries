# GTA 6 Trophies and Achievements: All 55 and How to Get Them

The full list of 55 trophies and achievements, grouped so story spoilers stay out of the way, with the quickest route to each.

Category

Trophy list

## The short version

- 55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze. Xbox has the same list as achievements, without the Platinum.
- No online trophies. Everything is earned in the single-player game.
- No difficulty trophies. The game has no difficulty settings.
- One playthrough is enough. You keep playing after the story ends.
- About 200 hours for the Platinum, according to the first full trophy guide. Most of that is 100% Completion.

## Before you start

- Cheats switch off trophies and saving while they are active, according to the same guide.
- Missions cannot be replayed and there is no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of each chapter. There are 15 manual save slots.
- One trophy may be missable: Positive Mental Attitude. The first meditation is reported to be available only during Chapter 1. Do it when Lucia offers it.
- 28 of the 55 are hidden on the console until you earn them. They are marked Hidden in the list.

## A sensible order

1. Play the story and take optional missions as they appear. Scan vehicles and study animals as you go, because both count toward 100%.
2. Work through 100% Completion once the credits roll. This is the long part.
3. Finish the stranger strands and anything left over.

Six hidden trophies still have no confirmed method. They are marked “not confirmed yet” and will be updated as guides test them.
