# GTA 6 Trophy Guide and Platinum Roadmap

November 18, 2026 • written by Krist Duro

Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 if you’re going for the GTA 6 Platinum. It’s the one early activity PowerPyx flags as potentially required for a trophy. The rest of your run can largely follow the story at your own pace, with postgame free roam for the long completion checklist.

This Duuro roadmap draws on PowerPyx’s GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap. Credit to PowerPyx for documenting the unlock conditions, completion thresholds, and Chapter 1 warning. Their guide has the full trophy-by-trophy walkthrough.

## Before you leave Chapter 1

After Down to Earth, check Jason’s kitchen in the morning. PowerPyx encountered Lucia’s meditation invitation there after that mission and notes that the timing may vary. When Lucia heads to the balcony, follow her, accept the invitation, and finish the meditation before moving on to Chapter 2.

This interaction advances the meditation counter, and PowerPyx is still checking whether skipping it blocks Positive Mental Attitude. The 100% checklist only asks for 15 meditations, so the possible missable concerns the trophy for meditating across Leonida. Keep a separate Chapter 1 save as insurance. ## What’s worth doing during the story

Keep a manual save around chapter transitions. With mission replay unavailable, an earlier save gives you a way back if you want to revisit a chapter. Main missions have no gold medal objectives to chase.

Use travel between missions to make a dent in three of the bigger counters. Scan unfamiliar vehicles in WAiNK, including boats and aircraft, toward the 200 base-model scans. Use binoculars to study wildlife and pets toward 100 species and breeds. Pick up optional jobs that fit your route toward the 100-task target. Each of those totals can be finished later, and work done during the story already counts.

## Use the 100% checklist to plan postgame cleanup

Once the story is complete, open Progress → Stats → 100% Completion. That’s the checklist for A Long Road. Use it as your starting point when planning a trip across Leonida so you can clear nearby activities in the same outing.
