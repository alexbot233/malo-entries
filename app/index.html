Keep a clean trophy save. Cheats disable trophies and saving. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so use the 15 manual slots: one at the start of each chapter, one before the finale, and rotate the rest.

  1. 01

    Story first, with the one early detour

    Play the five chapters for the automatic story trophies and the final-mission gold. In Chapter 1, after Down to Earth, check Jason's kitchen in the morning and take Lucia's balcony meditation before Chapter 2 — it is the only clearly missable step. Grab the timed Yago race the moment its hourglass appears, and keep chapter saves because missions cannot be replayed.

  2. 02

    100% completion cleanup

    After the credits, open Progress → Stats → 100% Completion and work the checklist in batches. Aim for 70 of 80 Challenges, 300 Stunts, all 33 underwater discoveries, all 42 shooting-range golds, 15 of 18 card sets, 200 WAiNK scans, 100 species, 100 optional jobs and 15 meditations. Earlier work counts, so sweep nearby objectives in the same outing.

  3. 03

    Counters and odd trophies last

    Finish the eight stranger strands, the contact finales and the looser counters such as races, robberies, vehicles, garages and spending. Positive Mental Attitude needs all 18 meditations even though 100% asks for only 15, and Cunning Stunts pops at 250 while 100% wants 300. When the last bronze lands, Vice Got a Price! follows automatically.

Type the number you have reached in the game into each box. Your counts are saved locally with the rest of your progress; the bars show how close each target is.
